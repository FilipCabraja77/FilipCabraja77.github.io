About the course

Open Water Scuba Diver

Your underwater adventure begins! Imagine watching the water move up over your mask as you slowly go below the surface - and continue to breathe!

It’s the most exciting feeling imaginable and yours when you become a NAUI Scuba Diver! Scuba Diver is our entry level scuba certification course, in which you will gain the fundamental knowledge and skills to safely scuba dive in open water.

When you complete your class, you and your buddy will be free to explore and discover the many wonders of the undersea world... and you will have earned NAUI Scuba Diver certification card - the most respected certification card in the world! Come and dive in... a lifetime of fun, adventure, memories, experiences, and new friends awaits you!

Step by step

Course content

  1. 1

    Getting started

    From age 10 and in good health, you can enrol in the NAUI Scuba Diver course.

  2. 2

    Theory & knowledge

    Your instructor teaches you about diving gear, the science of diving, and responsible diving practices and the environment.

  3. 3

    In-water skills

    Through practical exercises you learn many skills in the water for your safety and comfort.

  4. 4

    Open-water dives

    You complete your training with a minimum of four open-water dives - 6 dives in total, to a maximum depth of 18 m.

  5. International certification

    NAUI Scuba Diver certification card

Frequently asked questions

You can enroll from age 10, as long as you are in good health.

The course includes 6 dives to a maximum depth of 18 m, with a minimum of four open-water dives.

You earn the NAUI Scuba Diver certification card, an internationally respected certification.

You can continue with an Advanced Scuba Diver or Rescue Scuba Diver course, and progress toward Divemaster or even Instructor.
